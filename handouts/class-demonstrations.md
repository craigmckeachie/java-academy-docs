# Class Demonstrations

On project demonstration day, you have **10 minutes** to demonstrate your project to the class. During that time, you will:

1. **Present your application:** run through the different screens and scenarios.
2. **Describe and show one interesting piece of code** that you wrote.
3. **Explain a method:** your instructor will pick a method from your code, ask you to explain it, and ask a few questions about it.
4. **Answer questions from the audience** if time permits.

## Explaining a method

This isn't a trick, and it's not about finding mistakes. Your instructor wants to hear that you wrote your code and understand it. Everyone gets the same kinds of questions:

1. What does this method do, in one sentence?
2. What goes in, what comes out, and where is it called from?
3. Walk me through it with one real example.
4. What would happen if…? Why did you write it this way?

If you don't know an answer, say so and work it out: read the line, or run it in the debugger. That's a good answer too.
